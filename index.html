Fantasy Hoops Lab

Custom fantasy basketball tools for one league. Each page of this site helps me make one decision of the season: how my roster fits the playoffs, who to add, and which trade to make.

The pages

Roster

Who is on my team, and how many playoff games each player can start.

The players are in my custom-rank order. Each row has the season stats per game. For each playoff week (weeks 19 to 21), the page counts the games that each player can start in the best lineup. A day grid shows who fits the lineup on each of the 21 playoff days. The last rows compare the team starts with the games of my players and with the ideal count.

Adds plan

How to use the 5 adds of this fantasy week.

The planner finds the open starting slots on each day of the week. It recommends the NBA teams and the positions to add in each stream spot, and the day to add them. It does not pick the player. The week grid shows each add and the days that the new player starts. Above the grid, the matchup table shows the category totals of my team and my opponent.

Free agents

The player for each add.

The table lists the free agents of the last planning run, with their value, their recent stats and their games on each day. Filter by day, position and team, then sort by value or by games. Flags mark an unusual value (FA?), a rise in minutes (MIN+) and a role up (UP). A click on an add in the Adds plan opens this page with those filters set. The page also lists the waiver players.

Trades

1-for-1 trades that help in the playoffs.

The trade tool suggests 1-for-1 trades with the other teams. Each suggestion raises my started games in playoff weeks 19 to 21 and keeps my season value. The starts count my 8 kept players. The page also shows the trade deadline. Under the table, a trade builder checks a custom trade, for example 2-for-1, in the browser.

About this project

Fantasy basketball is one of my favorite hobbies, and this site is where I play with it. Fantasy Hoops Lab started as a set of custom draft rankings for my league. It grew into a site with one page for each big decision of the season.

The public apps rank players on all 9 categories. My team punts 3 of them: FG%, FT% and TO. I wanted rankings and plans built for the 6 categories that I try to win. Each tool reads my real league and does the math in Python. Then it puts the result here, where I can open it at my desk or on my phone.

These are the tools for one team in one league. The settings, the punted categories and the strategy are mine. You are welcome to read the code and take ideas from it.

How it works

  1. Data. The tools read my league from the league site. The NBA rosters, the schedule and the season stats come from public NBA stats. A season projections file gives the projected stats.
  2. Analysis. The Python package computes the player values, the lineups, the plans and the trades.
  3. Publish. The adds planner and the trade tool write static pages and push them to GitHub Pages.

The draft tools (the rankings, the draft board, an auction simulator and a keeper report) are local pages. They are not on this site.
