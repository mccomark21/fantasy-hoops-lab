How the plan works

This is one team in a Yahoo fantasy basketball league. Each day, the team starts up to 6 players. A player scores only on a day when his NBA team plays and he is in a starting slot.

The team keeps its core players all season. The other roster spots are stream spots. The league gives 5 adds each week, so the team can change the players in the stream spots.

The planner finds the days with open starting slots. For each stream spot, it finds the free agents who give the most started games with the fewest adds. Each row of the plans is one roster spot.

The value of a player is his value per game in the 6 categories that this league counts, above a replacement free agent: 0 is the value of the free agent at rank 15.
