Columns and marks

game in an open slot that he can fill game, no open slot for him. 2 second game of a back-to-back. Val: Season value, his custom value on the season-to-date box scores, blended with his projection, above a replacement free agent (0 = the free agent at rank 15 by Season value). A Val above 0 counts his share of games, as on the draft board. Next 7: blended value per game (Yahoo's projection for the next 7 days, moved toward the last 14 days) above a replacement free agent. Match: the Next 7 value with the matchup weights. A filter does not change Val, Next 7 or Match. Gms: his games on the selected days. FA? a value this high is unusual for a free agent. UP role up: a starter at his position is absent.

Min: his mean minutes in his last 5 games (L5) and in the last 14 days (14d), with the change. Starts: his starts / his games in the last 14 days. Closes: his closes / the close games of his team in the last 14 days. A close: he is on the floor at the end of a close game. The ESPN play-by-play gives these numbers. MIN+ L5 is 4 minutes or more above 14d. The word after Starts is the starter flag, from his last 10 games (5 or more). The word after Closes is the closer flag, from the last 8 close games of his team (4 or more). A close game that he did not play counts as not closed. New: he started each of his last 3 games (closed each of the last 2 close games), and fewer than half of the earlier games in the window. Regular: 70% or more. Inconsistent: 20% or more. No word: less, or too few games. They do not change Val.

Mon open: he has a game next Monday 11/9, and your core players leave a slot open that he can fill (1 open slots). Tue open: he has a game next Tuesday 11/10, and your core players leave a slot open that he can fill (4 open slots). The mark changes no value and no plan.
