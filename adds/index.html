How to read the Skyline

The skyline at the top shows the NBA games of each night. A blue night is a light night: 6 or fewer games. The ladder below has one rung for each number of adds. Rung n is the best plan for your stream spots with n adds. A white block starts on an add day and runs to the next add day of the rung. It names the teams of the adds of its first day. A dot marks a day that a new player of the rung starts. A line between 2 dots marks a back-to-back. With 5 teams or fewer, the block names the teams. Else it gives the count. The number at the left is the started games of your stream spots. "+1" marks a game that the extra add buys. RE-PLAN: the rung does not keep the adds of the rung above. A click on a block opens the Free agents page with the filters of the add.

Val = season value to date, blended with the projection until 10 games. Stream spots: your lowest players by Val.

How the plan works

This is one team in a Yahoo fantasy basketball league. Each day, the team starts up to 6 players. A player scores only on a day when his NBA team plays and he is in a starting slot.

The team keeps its core players all season. The other roster spots are stream spots. The league gives 5 adds each week, so the team can change the players in the stream spots.

The planner finds the days with open starting slots. For each stream spot, it finds the NBA teams whose games fill the most open slots with the fewest adds. You pick the player in the free-agent table.

The value of a player (Val) is his season value to date per game in the 6 categories that this league counts, blended with his projection in his first games, above a replacement free agent: 0 is the value of the free agent at rank 15. The stream spots hold your lowest players by Val.
