How to read the plan

Games: the NBA games of the day. A deeper shade: more games. An add sits on its add day: the slots that the new player can fill, then the NBA teams that fit equally. With 6 teams or more, the cell gives the count. Util: any position. A later add in a row drops the player of the earlier add. A band after an add shows the days that the new player holds the spot, up to the next add in the row or Sunday. A square on the band marks a day that he starts: his team plays, and an open slot takes him. Each add also starts on its add day. A click on an add or a day opens the Free agents page with the filters of the add or the day. A past day is muted. A dashed cell shows the add that the last run on or before that day recommended for it.

How the plan works

This is one team in a Yahoo fantasy basketball league. Each day, the team starts up to 6 players. A player scores only on a day when his NBA team plays and he is in a starting slot.

The team keeps its core players all season. The other roster spots are stream spots. The league gives 5 adds each week, so the team can change the players in the stream spots.

The planner finds the days with open starting slots. For each stream spot, it finds the NBA teams whose games fill the most open slots with the fewest adds. You pick the player in the free-agent table.

The value of a player is his value per game in the 6 categories that this league counts, above a replacement free agent: 0 is the value of the free agent at rank 15.
