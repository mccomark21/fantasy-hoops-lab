# Team plan of 2026-10-28

On 2026-10-28, the owner decided that the adds planner stops recommending players. It recommends NBA teams and positions for the stream spots. Source: #140 and its Decision comments.

## Why

The owner said that the planning run "takes too long and really isn't worth it". The step times in #140 show that in full-week runs, the player searches took about 15 of 42 seconds. Most of the rest was the Yahoo reads. So #140 makes a run about one third shorter, not instant.

## Design history (2026-10-28)

1. First brief: a chain of moves for each stream spot ("Mon add a G from CHA, Thu drop him, add a C from DET"), 6 or more teams as a count, and a click sets the free-agent filters. #131 and #112 closed as not planned.
2. Prototypes A (ledger) and B (grid of bars): the owner rejected both.
3. The owner gave the layout: always Mon to Sun horizontally, a Games row (NBA games of the day), an Open row, and 2 Add rows with cells such as "+G/F SAC" or "+Util 8 teams".
4. Three looks of that layout (C1 quiet, C2 scoreboard, C3 heat). The owner picked C3, removed the Open row (the shading comes from the games only), and asked that past days show their recommendations muted.
5. Fixes from the owner's review of the sample: a 4-team list ran out of its cell (now a fit check from 320 to 1440 px); dark text on a dark selected cell (now a 4.5:1 contrast check in each state); a past day keeps the last recommendation made on or before it.
6. The owner asked to show that an added player holds the spot for several days: a band through the held days, with a square on each start day.
7. The owner approved the sample with the long legend collapsed, and asked for the build.

## Process note

The owner rule "show a sample first" ([decision of #75](run-issues-73-to-76.md)) paid off here. The owner rejected the first two directions, and five review rounds changed the design before any code. The fit check and the contrast check came from the owner's own findings. Both checks are in the acceptance criteria of #140 as page tests.
