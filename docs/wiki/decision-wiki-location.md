# Decision: the wiki lives in docs/wiki/

## Decision

On 2026-10-21, the owner decided to put the project wiki in `docs/wiki/`.

## Scope

- The wiki records history and decisions, with a date on each claim.
- The tool docs stay in `docs/<tool>/README.md`. The shared terms stay in [docs/glossary.md](../glossary.md). The wiki links them and does not copy them.
- The ADRs stay in `docs/adr/`.
