# Basketball Monster terms of 2026-11-18

On 2026-11-18, the owner asked to set up the Basketball Monster (BBM) sign-in, read the BBM data and find improvements to the tools. Issue #248 has the plan of 2026-11-15.

## Finding of 2026-11-18

On 2026-11-18, a research agent read https://basketballmonster.com/termsofuse.aspx (Centef LLC). The terms say:

- "You may not scrape or otherwise copy our Content without permission. You agree ... not to use any data mining, data gathering or extraction method."
- "You may not, for example, republish any portion of the Content on any Internet, Intranet or extranet site or incorporate the Content in any database, compilation, archive or cache."
- Use is "solely for your personal, non-commercial use". A single download or print copy is allowed for personal use.

The agent read the terms this way: an automated read breaks the plain text, and derived values on the public site come near "republish". That is not legal advice. `robots.txt` blocks nothing. The subscriber pages redirect to `default.aspx` without a sign-in.

This finding conflicts with the 2026-11-15 plan of #248 (an automated reader, public derived values). It also conflicts with the rule of 2026-10-26 in [Bluesky streaming posts](bluesky-streaming-posts.md): inputs must come from sources whose terms allow automated use.
