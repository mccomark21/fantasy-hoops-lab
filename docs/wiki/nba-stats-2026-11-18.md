### Proposed luck math

The builder of #254 may change it, with tests, and must record each change here.

- Categories with a chance stat: STL (deflections), BLK (contested 2-point shots, with rim FGA defended as the alternative, superseded by the table decision below), AST (potential assists), REB (rebound chances) and 3PM (3PA from box scores). PTS has no chance stat and is out.
- n = season chances. y = season makes.
- Baseline rate p0 = (y_prev + k x league) / (n_prev + k), from the 2025-26 row of the player. With no 2025-26 row, p0 = the league rate. Without this baseline, elite shot-blockers (Wembanyama, Clingan, Thybulle) look "lucky" against a league rate.
- Expected = n x p0. Luck z = (y - n x p0) / sqrt(n x p0 x (1 - p0)). A negative z is unlucky.
- Overall luck = (sum of the category z) / sqrt(count), over the ready categories (Stouffer). A category is ready only with enough games and chances.
- The settings go in a `[players.luck]` section: k for each category, the minimum games and the minimum chances.
- #253 proposal: shrink toward the expected value when the category is ready, else toward the projection. A blend of the two is the alternative.

The owner rule "check value before building" applies. #254 shows the owner a static sample table of real data before the page build.

## Build of #254, stage 1 (2026-11-20)

The module `players/luck.py` and the `[players.luck]` section of `config.toml` are on the branch `254-luck-module-and-luck-tab`. The Luck tab waits for the owner to approve the sample.

### The rebound chances

- Rebounds for each minute did not change: 0.190 in 2025-26 and 0.190 in 2026-27.
- Rebound chances for each minute went up from 0.273 to 0.352 (+29%). Offensive and defensive chances both went up.
- So NBA.com probably counts more rebound chances in 2026-27. The player data does not show a real change of the rebound rate.
- With the raw 2025-26 baseline, the mean REB z of the players with 30 chances or more was -3.4. With the rescale, it was -0.07.

### Changes to the proposed luck math

1. The baseline is multiplied by league rate now / league rate 2025-26, in each category. With no 2025-26 row, p0 = the league rate now. Reason: the REB count change above.
2. The z uses the beta-binomial spread: n x p0 x (1 - p0) x (w + n) / (w + 1), with w = n_prev + k. The added part is the doubt about p0. On the data of 2026-11-20, it lowered the spread of the z from 1.24-1.43 to 1.11-1.32 (3PM: 1.06 to 1.01). A spread of 1 is the target.
3. k is a fit to the 2025-26 rates (method of moments, players with 100 chances or more), rounded: STL 80, BLK 25, AST 100, REB 60, 3PM 300 chances.
4. Ready: 5 games, and STL 20, BLK 25, AST 40, REB 40, 3PM 20 chances. These are agent defaults. The owner did not approve them yet.

### The sample

On the data of 2026-11-20, against the league rate, Wembanyama had a BLK z of +6.5 and Thybulle +7.3. Against their own baselines, Wembanyama had -1.0, and Thybulle was not ready (15 contested 2-point shots). The z of each category still has a spread above 1. Role changes since last season are a probable cause.
