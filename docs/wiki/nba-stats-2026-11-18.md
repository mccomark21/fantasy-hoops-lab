# NBA hustle and tracking stats of 2026-11-18

## The owner request

On 2026-11-18 the owner asked if the NBA stats API gives stats that the box score does not show, such as contested shots and deflections. The owner then asked for a sample, and then for issues for the most useful metrics.

## The sample

A scratch script read 12 league-wide tables of the 2026-27 season with the `nba_api` package. It ran from the owner's computer. All 12 requests passed.

- 399 of the 500 players in `data/yahoo_players.csv` matched by name. The other 101 players had no NBA row and no row in `data/box_scores.csv`.
- For the 220 players with 20 or more minutes per game, these were the correlations:

| Stat | Category | Correlation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deflections | STL | 0.78 |
| Contested 2-point shots | BLK | 0.74 (215 players) |
| Rim shots defended | BLK | 0.63 (215 players) |
| Potential assists | AST | 0.96 |
| Rebound chances | REB | 0.95 |

About 10 to 15 games had been played. The numbers are noisy.
